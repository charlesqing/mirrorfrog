**In 2026, AI compute growth has hit a hard constraint — power.** When NVIDIA Rubin NVL576 hits **1 MW per rack**, the xAI Colossus cluster **200 MW**, and OpenAI's planned Stargate campus **5 GW**, **power supply is becoming the biggest bottleneck for AI development**. This article analyzes this "power crisis" and its solutions in depth.

## Power Demand: Exponential Growth

### Single-Rack Power Progression

| Year | Representative rack | Single-rack power | Cluster scale | Total power |

| 2020 | DGX A100 (8 GPU) | 6.5 kW | 100-1,000 | 0.7-7 MW |

> **Single-rack power grew 150× in 5 years** (6.5 kW → 1 MW). This is approaching **nuclear reactor** output.

## Three Major Power Crises

### Crisis 1: Insufficient Data Center Power Supply

#### United States

- **2025 Northern Virginia** (world's largest data center cluster): **power applications face a 3-5 year wait**
- **Texas Round Rock** (Oracle HQ): **grid capacity full**
- **PJM grid** (largest US regional grid): **5-10 GW shortfall 2026-2030**

#### China

- **Inner Mongolia, Guizhou** (government-subsidized data centers): **partial power rationing**
- **Beijing, Shanghai** (tier-1 cities): **only approve new builds at PUE below 1.4**
- **Data center power quota**: **60% already used by end of 2025**

#### Europe

- **Amsterdam, Netherlands** (MSFT investment zone): **paused new data center approvals**
- **Dublin, Ireland** (AWS European HQ): **no approvals before 2030**
- **Nordics** (Norway, Sweden, Finland): **rich renewables but limited capacity**

### Crisis 2: Soaring Power Costs

| Region | 2020 industrial price | 2025 industrial price | Increase |
|--------|----------------------|----------------------|----------|
| US (Virginia) | $0.05/kWh | $0.08/kWh | +60% |
| Germany | $0.18/kWh | $0.35/kWh | +94% |
| UK | $0.20/kWh | $0.40/kWh | +100% |
| Japan | $0.18/kWh | $0.30/kWh | +67% |
| China (West) | $0.04/kWh | $0.06/kWh | +50% |

> **European electricity prices doubled in 2 years** — one reason AI companies are migrating to the US/Middle East.

### Crisis 3: Carbon Emissions and ESG Pressure

- **2025 global data center carbon**: **~150 Mt CO2** (million tonnes)
- **2028 (E)**: **~400 Mt CO2** (exceeds all of Germany's annual emissions)
- **ESG funds**: increasingly require "100% renewable" data centers

## Response: The Nuclear Renaissance

### 1. Nuclear Restarts

#### US TVA + Microsoft

- **Oct 2025 agreement**: Microsoft restarts **Three Mile Island Unit 1** (840 MW)
- **2028 online**: dedicated power for Microsoft data centers
- **20-year contract**: all 835 MW consumed by Microsoft

#### Amazon + Talen Energy

- **Mar 2025 agreement**: Amazon acquires Talen Energy's **Susquehanna nuclear plant** 960 MW data center campus
- **960 MW fully powers** AWS
- **First nuclear-direct data center**

#### Google + Kairos Power (Small Modular Reactor SMR)

- **May 2025 agreement**: 500 MW SMR powers Google data centers
- **First batch 2030**: 500 MW
- **2035 total**: 500 MW × N modules

### 2. Small Modular Reactors (SMR)

**SMR (Small Modular Reactor)** is the **ultimate solution** for AI data centers:

| Item | Traditional nuclear | SMR |
|------|---------------------|-----|
| Single-unit capacity | 1,000-1,600 MW | 50-300 MW |
| Construction period | 7-10 years | 3-4 years |
| Investment | $10B+ | $1-2B |
| Flexibility | Low | High (scalable) |
| Safety | High | Higher (passive safety) |
| Siting | Strict | Flexible (factory-built) |

#### Major SMR Suppliers

| Vendor | Model | Capacity | 2026 status | Main customers |
|--------|-------|----------|-------------|----------------|
| **NuScale** | VOYGR | 77 MW module | **First 2027** | UAMPS (cancelled), Romania |
| **Rolls-Royce** | UK SMR | 470 MW | First 2030 | UK government |
| **TerraPower** | Natrium | 345 MW | First 2030 | Bill Gates + Warren Buffett |
| **X-energy** | Xe-100 | 80 MW module | First 2028 | Amazon + Energy Northwest |
| **Kairos Power** | KP-FHR | 140 MW module | First 2030 | Google + TVA |
| **Holtec** | SMR-160 | 160 MW | First 2029 | Multiple US utilities |
| **CNNC (China)** | HTR-PM | 250 MW module | **Connected to grid 2023** | Shandong, China |

> **CNNC HTR-PM connected to grid in 2023** — the world's **first commercial SMR**, 4-5 years ahead of US SMRs.

### 3. Other Clean Energy

#### Solar + Storage

- **2025 largest US solar project**: **Sunlight Captive 1.4 GW** (Texas) + 700 MWh storage
- **Microsoft / Google / Amazon** all signed PPAs (power purchase agreements)
- **Limitation**: unstable at night / cloudy, high storage cost

#### Geothermal

- **Google + Fervo Energy** (Nov 2025): **150 MW geothermal** powers Nevada data centers
- **2028 plan**: 500 MW
- **Advantage**: 24/7 stable supply

#### Wind

- **Amazon + Avangrid** (2025): **enough 1.5 GW wind** to power Texas data centers
- **Limitation**: intermittent

### 4. Free Cooling + Liquid Cooling

**Reducing data center PUE (Power Usage Effectiveness)** is also key:

| Cooling method | PUE | Suitable region |
|----------------|-----|-----------------|
| **Liquid cooling (DLC)** | **1.05-1.15** | Any region |
| Indirect evaporative | 1.15-1.25 | Cold regions |
| Traditional air | 1.4-1.6 | Any region |
| Free cooling (Nordics) | 1.02-1.05 | Cold regions |

> **Liquid cooling PUE 1.05-1.15 vs air 1.4-1.6** — **25-40% energy savings**. NVIDIA Rubin NVL576 requires liquid cooling.

## Green AI Strategy

### 1. Energy Efficiency Optimization

- **FP4 / FP8 quantization**: 50% less power vs FP16
- **Sparse compute**: 2:4 sparsity cuts 50% compute
- **Liquid cooling**: reduces PUE 25-40%
- **Model compression**: MoE, Distillation, Pruning

## Future Outlook

## Conclusion

The **"next battlefield" of AI compute is power**:

1. **Single-rack power breaks 1 MW** (Rubin NVL576) — approaching nuclear reactors
2. **Global AI data centers need 800 TWh in 2026** — more than all of Germany
3. **Nuclear restarts**: Microsoft / Amazon / Google each lock in 1-2 GW of nuclear
4. **SMR rise**: first commercial 2028, 50-300 MW per unit
5. **Renewables**: solar / wind / geothermal / hydro + storage
6. **Liquid cooling standard**: PUE 1.05-1.15 vs air 1.4-1.6

**AI compute without power is a castle in the air.**
