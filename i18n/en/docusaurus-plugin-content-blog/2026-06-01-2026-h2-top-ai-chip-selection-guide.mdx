## Conclusion

Core principles of 2026 H2 selection:

1. **Training + inference = same chip?** Most scenarios use NVIDIA B300 Ultra / H200 for both.
2. **Ultra-low-latency inference?** Choose **Groq 3 LPX** — no substitute.
3. **AWS cloud?** Choose **Trainium 3** — 2-3× per dollar.
4. **Google Cloud?** Choose **TPU 8t (training) + TPU 8i (inference)**.
5. **China market?** **Huawei Ascend 920** + **CloudMatrix 384 Ultra**.
6. **Open ecosystem?** **AMD Helios** (UALoF open interconnect).
7. **Budget-sensitive?** **AMD MI300X** or **Intel Gaudi 3**.
8. **Local LLM?** **Apple M3 Ultra** (192GB UMA).

**There is no "best," only "most suitable."** Combine your model scale, latency requirements, budget, and region with the selection tree and cheat sheet in this article.
