## Investment Logic

### Why Startups Survive

1. **Niche markets**: government / defense / academic / special industries (where NVIDIA doesn't play)
2. **Differentiated architecture**: RISC-V / photonic / analog / dataflow (paths NVIDIA won't take)
3. **Localization**: China / Europe / Japan / India (data sovereignty + politics)
4. **Big-customer binding**: Tenstorrent + LG, SambaNova + US Air Force

### Why Startups Die

1. **NVIDIA ecosystem crush**: CUDA + cuDNN + TensorRT hard to surpass
2. **High capital burn**: 7nm tapeout $30M+, 5nm $80M+
3. **High software migration cost**: moving from CUDA to non-NVIDIA platforms takes 6-12 months
4. **High customer concentration**: losing a big customer = death

## Conclusion

AI chip startups face "winner-takes-all":

1. **Tenstorrent** most likely to succeed (Jim Keller + RISC-V + open-source)
2. **SambaNova** successful pivot (enterprise appliance + US government)
3. **Graphcore** acquired by SoftBank (fate handed to Japan)
4. **Cambricon / Moore Threads / Biren** benefit from China domestic substitution
5. **Lightmatter / Esperanto** survive in niche markets
6. **Mythic and other small companies** struggle to survive

**Over the next 5 years, the AI chip industry will experience "big fish eating small fish"** — startups will either IPO, be acquired, or disappear.
