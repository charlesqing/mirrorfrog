# 2026 H1 AI Chip Industry Review: Blackwell Ultra, China's Big Three, and the New Era of Inference

> In the first half of 2026, the AI chip industry underwent a historic turning point — the industry focus shifted from "training race" to "inference efficiency", domestic chip market share exceeded 40% for the first time, NVIDIA built higher barriers with Blackwell Ultra, and the inference-dedicated chip track blossomed.

---

## 1. Computing Power Doubles Again: NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra Launch (June 1)

On **June 1, 2026**, NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang unveiled the new generation AI chip **Blackwell Ultra** at **Computex 2026** in Taipei, setting a new starting line for the AI infrastructure competition in the next two years.

### Key Specifications

| Metric | Blackwell Ultra | B200 | Improvement |
|------|-----------------|-------|------|
| **FP8 Computing** | **20 petaFLOPS** | ~10 petaFLOPS | **100%** |
| **Architecture** | Blackwell Ultra | Blackwell | Upgrade |
| **Expected Delivery** | Q1 2027 | Q1 2026 | — |
| **Positioning** | Hyper-scale training + inference | Training + inference | Flagship |

### Industry Significance

- **100% FP8 computing improvement**: Blackwell Ultra doubles the FP8 computing compared to B200, reaching 20 petaFLOPS
- **2027 Q1 delivery**: Mass production and delivery expected in Q1 2027, providing a 1-2 year technology leadership window
- **HBM3e + 8 TB/s**: Maintains B200s memory bandwidth advantage while further optimizing power efficiency

### 2.2 Huawei Ascend: The Anchor of Domestic Computing Power

#### Market Position

- 2025 Ascend series shipment **812,000 units**, accounting for **49%** of domestic AI accelerator cards, firmly ranking first in China
- Ascend 950PR single card FP8 computing reaches **1 P** (PetaFLOPS), FP4 computing reaches **2 P**
- Inference performance is approximately **2.87x** that of NVIDIA H20, priced at only **72,000-75,000 yuan**, with significant cost-effectiveness advantage

#### Full-Stack Advantage

Huaweis "end-pipe-cloud-chip" integrated strategy is the core barrier of Ascend:
- **Chip design**: Da Vinci 3.0 architecture continuous iteration
- **Operating system**: Hongmeng/Euler OS deep optimization
- **Network communication**: Euler network protocol stack
- **Cloud service**: Huawei Cloud ModelArts platform seamless integration

#### Latest Progress

- June 5, 2026: Shenzhen Hetao Academy, together with HIT (Shenzhen) and Huawei team, completed **1.6 trillion parameter DeepSeek V4 Pro large model full-parameter post-training** relying on **Ascend 910C cluster**
- This is the **first time** domestic AI chips have completed trillion-parameter-level large model training, marking "domestic substitution" moving from inference to training

### 2.3 Cambricon: The First Profitable Domestic AI Chip Benchmark

#### Performance Explosion

| Metric | Full Year 2025 | Q1 2026 | YoY Growth |
|------|---------------|-------------|----------|
| **Revenue** | 6.497 billion yuan | 2.885 billion yuan | +453% / +160% |
| **Net Profit** | 2.059 billion yuan (first annual profit) | 1.013 billion yuan | — / +185% |

#### Core Product: Siyuan 590

- Under **DeepSeek R1 inference scenario**, TPS can reach **942**, about **50%** higher than H20
- Multi-year joint optimization with ByteDance, possessing the strongest cloud inference deployment capability in the short term
- In Q1 2026 revenue of 2.885 billion yuan, Siyuan 590 contributed over 70%

#### Potential Risks

**Absent** from the No. 2 《Safe and Reliable Assessment Result Announcement》 in 2026, the reason has not been clarified yet, which will impact its domestic government and enterprise market performance.

### 2.4 Tsing Micro: The "Third Route" of Reconfigurable Chips

#### Technology Route

Tsing Micros **RPU (Reconfigurable Processing Unit)** is a **software-defined hardware architecture** that can dynamically reconfigure hardware resources according to different AI models:

- **Scenario adaptation**: Optimize hardware resources for specific AI workloads
- **Inference cost advantage**: Reduce inference cost by **50%** compared to GPU
- **Flexible deployment**: Support cloud, edge, and terminal scenarios

## 3. Inference Chip Track Explosion: NVIDIA Acquires Groq, Specialized Chips Thrive

### 3.1 NVIDIA Acquires Groq (February 2026)

**February 2026**, NVIDIA announced the acquisition of **Groq** for **$20 billion**, a move that shocked the industry:

- **LPU (Language Processing Unit)**: Groqs dedicated inference chip, with latency 10x lower than GPU
- **Why acquire**: NVIDIA GPU excels at training, but lags in inference latency; Groq fills this gap
- **Strategic intent**: Build a full-stack advantage from training to inference

### 3.2 Cerebras: The First Listed Wafer-Scale Chip Company

**March 2026**, **Cerebras** was listed on Nasdaq through IPO, becoming the **first listed wafer-scale chip company** in the United States:

- **WSE-3**: 900mm² wafer-scale chip, 125 TFLOPS FP16, 40GB SRAM
- **Valuation**: $22 billion, reflecting the markets high recognition of wafer-scale chips
- **Positioning**: Dedicated for hyper-scale AI training and inference

### 3.3 Other Inference Chip Startups

| Company | Product | Characteristics |
|---------|---------|--------------|
| **Taalas** | HC1 | 3D stacked wafer-scale chip, 100 TFLOPS FP16, dedicated inference |
| **SambaNova** | SN40L | Reconfigurable dataflow architecture, optimized for inference |
| **Etched** | EtchedA | ASIC dedicated for Transformer models |

## 4. 2026 H1 Overall Pattern: Three Major Trends

### 4.1 Domestic Chips Enter "Mass Production Period"

- **Huawei Ascend 910C**: Mass production capacity ramping up, supporting trillion-parameter model training
- **Cambricon MLU690**: Launched, FP16 computing reaches 600 TFLOPS
- **Iluvatar BI-V150**: Mass production in 2024, compatible with CUDA ecosystem

### 4.2 Capitalization Wave Sweeps Domestic Chip Industry

- **Cambricon**: First annual profit, market value exceeding 300 billion yuan
- **Iluvatar CoreX**: IPO application submitted, expected to list in H2 2026
- **Enflame Technology**: Series D financing completed, preparing for IPO

### 4.3 Supply Chain Constraints Become the Biggest Bottleneck

- **HBM supply shortage**: SK Hynix, Samsung, and Micron capacity fully booked for 2026-2027
- **TSMC CoWoS capacity**: Monthly capacity reaching 40,000 wafers, still in short supply
- **Domestic substitution**: ChangXin Memory (CXMT) HBM2E mass production progress is key

## 5. 2026 H2 Outlook: Rubin Architecture, Ascend 950PR Launch, MI350X Delivery

### 5.1 NVIDIA Rubin Architecture (Expected Q4 2026)

- **3nm process**: TSMC 3nm process, further improving power efficiency
- **HBM4**: Adopting HBM4 memory, bandwidth expected to reach 10 TB/s
- **FP4 optimization**: Further optimizing FP4 computing efficiency, inference performance improved by 2x

### 5.2 Huawei Ascend 950PR Mass Production (Expected Q3 2026)

- **FP8 1 PFLOPS**: Single card FP8 computing reaching 1 PetaFLOPS
- **Inference dedicated**: Optimized for inference scenarios, performance exceeding H20 by 2.87x
- **Software ecosystem**: CANN 8.0 release, further improving inference performance

### 5.3 AMD MI350X Delivery (Expected Q3 2026)

- **FP16 2600 TFLOPS**: Approaching Blackwell performance
- **288GB HBM3e**: Large memory capacity, supporting massive model inference
- **Price advantage**: Pricing 20-30% lower than NVIDIA, attracting price-sensitive customers

## 6. Conclusion: The Dividends of the Inference Era, and the Arrival of Diverse Ecology

The first half of 2026 was a historic turning point for the AI chip industry:

1. **Industry focus shift**: From "training race" to "inference efficiency"
2. **Domestic substitution acceleration**: Market share exceeding 40%, "available" to "good"
3. **Diversified ecology**: Wafer-scale chips, reconfigurable chips, ASIC dedicated chips blossoming
4. **Supply chain constraints**: HBM and CoWoS capacity becoming the biggest bottleneck

**Future outlook**:
- **Short term (2026 H2)**: Inference chips will continue to be the focus, with NVIDIA, AMD, and domestic manufacturers all increasing investment
- **Medium term (2027-2028)**: Rubin architecture launch, domestic chips further narrowing the gap
- **Long term (2029+)**: Quantum computing, optical computing, and other next-generation computing technologies gradually maturing
