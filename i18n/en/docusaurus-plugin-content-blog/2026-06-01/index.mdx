# 2026 Global AI Computing Power Report & Top 10 Trends Released

On May 29, 2026, during the **2026 World Intelligent Industry Expo** in Tianjin, the **China Intelligent Computing Industry Alliance, National Supercomputing Center Tianjin, Tianjin Artificial Intelligence Society, Shenzhen Artificial Intelligence Industry Association, Top Tech, and Top Think Tank** jointly released the "**2026 Global AI Computing Power Development Research Report**".

The report provides an in-depth analysis of the current status and future trends of the global AI computing power industry, revealing that the computing power industry has entered a new development stage of "**Intelligent Computing Drive, System Reconstruction**".

## Core Perspectives

### 1. Computing Power Becomes a National Strategic Element

The global computing power industry is entering a new development stage of "Intelligent Computing Drive, System Reconstruction". With the rise of "**Token Economy**", computing power has become a key foundational element supporting national technological breakthroughs, industrial competition, and strategic布局.

Computing power is evolving from traditional IT support to a **strategic foundation** driving technological innovation and industrial revolution.

### 2. AI Computing Power Development Covers the Entire Technology Stack

AI computing power development requires full-link upgrades covering **chips, complete machines, and computing clusters**, while also matching the differentiated computing power demands of model training, inference, and data preparation.

- **Training side**: Pre-training of ultra-large models requires **ten-thousand-card level** computing power support
- **Inference side**: Ultra-large models require **thousand-card** computing power
- **Data preparation**: Requires **tens to hundreds of cards** computing power scale

Computing power demand on both training and inference ends will continue to grow.

### 3. Domestic AI Chip Industry's Distinctive Path

The domestic AI chip industry follows a path of "**independent control + cluster breakthrough + software-hardware integration + cost-performance advantage**", which differs from the foreign approach of pursuing absolute single-chip computing power, and better matches the demands of large-scale computing power deployment.

### 4. Computing Center Energy Challenges and Solutions

Computing centers have become the fastest-growing area of electricity demand globally. In the future, it will be necessary to build a diversified energy supply system of "**short-term wind-solar-storage integration, medium-term nuclear energy, long-term hydrogen energy**".

Meanwhile, **space computing power** will become a new direction to solve ground computing power bottlenecks.

### 5. Computing-Network Integration Becomes the Core Direction

Future computing power will develop toward "**computing-network integration**", enabling computing power to be available on-demand like water and electricity, becoming a core component of the national modern infrastructure system.

The computing network has been included in the major engineering projects of the national "**15th Five-Year Plan**", standing alongside public infrastructure such as water and electricity as a core component of the modern infrastructure system.

### Global Computing Center Capacity and Energy Consumption Forecast

- **Global computing center total capacity**: Expected to grow from **102GW** in 2026 to **220GW** in 2030
  - AI load capacity increases from **62GW** to **156GW**, proportion increasing to **71%**

- **US computing center annual power consumption**: Expected to grow from **292TWh** to **606TWh**, proportion of total US electricity demand increasing to **11%**

- **China computing center total capacity**: Expected to approach **60GW** in 2030, AI load proportion increasing to **48%**

- **Global computing center power consumption**: According to IEA baseline scenario forecast, will grow from approximately **415TWh** in 2024 to approximately **945TWh** in 2030, with an average annual growth rate of about **15%**

### Embodied AI Computing Power Support Data

- **Cloud computing power**: Can achieve daily generation of **PB-level** interactive data, large model training cycles shortened from monthly to **weekly**
- **Edge computing power**: **Tens to hundreds of TOPS** computing power can complete 10-50ms low-latency real-time perception decision-making

## Industry Trend Analysis

### Computing Technology Architecture Trends

#### 1. Heterogeneous Computing Architecture Upgrade

Evolving from traditional CPU+GPU architecture to **GPU+LPU+CPU+DPU** new heterogeneous inference architecture.

CPU plays a core role in task scheduling, data preprocessing, serial task processing, and system interconnection in heterogeneous architectures. In 2010, "**Tianhe-1A**" pioneered the large-scale deployment of CPU+GPU architecture, leading the global intelligent computing underlying architecture direction.

#### 2. Clear Computing Power Expansion Paths

- **Scale Up (Vertical Expansion)**: Pursuing ultimate performance by improving single-node hardware configuration
- **Scale Out (Horizontal Expansion)**: Achieving load sharing and high availability by adding nodes

Both together constitute the core support of computing system capabilities.

#### 3. Super-Node Servers Become Mainstream

Possessing advantages of **ultra-high interconnection bandwidth and low communication latency**, can shorten model training cycles.

**Representative products**:
- Huawei Ascend 384 Super-Node
- Sugon scaleX640 Super-Node
- Alibaba Cloud Panjiu AL128 Super-Node
- Inspur Yuannao SD200
- Kunlunxin Super-Node Solution

#### 4. Long-Context Processing Technology Optimization

Through the synergy of **Compressed Sparse Attention (CSA), Heavy-Compressed Attention (HCA)** and sliding window mechanisms, building a "coarse-grained + fine-grained, sparse + dense" long-context modeling system, improving computing power usage efficiency.

**Representative application**: DeepSeek-V4's attention architecture design.

### AI Computing Power Key Domain Development Trends

#### AI Chip Domain

**International vendors**:
- **NVIDIA**: Leads high-end training and inference markets with Blackwell and Rubin architectures
- **Google**: Deepens software-hardware vertical integration with self-developed TPU
- **AWS**: Provides cost-effective cloud computing power solutions through Trainium training chips and Inferentia inference chips

**Domestic vendors**:
Forming a product matrix represented by **Huawei Ascend 910C, Kunlunxin P800, Moore Threads MTT S5000, and Muxi Xiyun C600**.

In 2026, Huawei released the "**Tao (τ) Law**", aiming to systematically reduce time constants, improving transistor density through technologies such as logic folding, promoting domestic chip technology evolution.

#### AI Workstation Domain

- **Form coverage**: Divided into tower, mobile, and mini三类, adapting to different deployment scenarios
- **Computing power grade coverage**: Divided into entry-level, professional-level, and enterprise-level三类, covering full-scenario AI computing power demands from personal development to enterprise-level deployment

#### AI Server Domain

- Can be divided into **training AI servers** and **inference AI servers** by function
- Can be divided into **cloud AI servers** and **edge AI servers** by deployment method

Equipped with capabilities such as high computing power output, high memory bandwidth, and high-speed interconnection, adapting to large-scale parallel computing tasks.

#### AI Computing Center Domain

- Presenting development trends of "**high AI proportion, high power density, high power consumption**"
- Ultra-large-scale AI computing centers become construction focus
- Energy supply developing toward **diversified clean** direction

**Space computing power** becomes a new direction, which can leverage environmental advantages of continuous light exposure, extreme cold vacuum, and no atmospheric interference in space to solve energy, heat dissipation, and interconnection bottlenecks of ground computing centers.

Currently, **Starcloud Company and Guoxing Aerospace** have carried out preliminary explorations.

### Computing Power Application Scenario Trends

#### 1. Scientific Research Paradigm Transformation

"**Dry-Wet Closed Loop**" research paradigm becomes mainstream, forming a closed loop through data feedback between AI-driven "dry experiments" and automated experimental verification "wet experiments", promoting scientific research to shift from experience-driven to model-driven.

#### 2. Synthetic Biology Empowerment

AI's multi-task learning and unknown space exploration capabilities can decipher the complex mapping of biological systems "**sequence—structure—function**", achieving application breakthroughs in protein synthesis, gene editing, and nucleic acid vaccines.

For example, **AlphaFold series models** achieve revolutionary breakthroughs in protein structure prediction.

#### 3. Embodied Intelligence Support

Efficient collaboration between cloud and terminal computing power provides full-stack computing power support for embodied intelligence, covering the full-link closed loop of massive data processing, high-fidelity simulation, model training, and terminal real-time perception decision-making.

### Computing Power Infrastructure Development Trends

**Computing-network integration** becomes the core direction, evolving from "interconnect first, then form a network" to a national integrated computing network.

The three major telecom operators have carried out interconnection work between their own computing power and nationally dispersed social computing power, promoting universal computing power supply.

### Industry Ecosystem Trends

The domestic computing power ecosystem continues to improve, with deepening collaboration between government, enterprises, academia, and research. The China Intelligent Computing Industry Alliance, National Supercomputing Center Tianjin, local AI societies, industry associations, and industrial service institutions jointly build exchange platforms to promote computing power technology R&D, standard formulation, achievement transformation, and talent cultivation, helping the high-quality development of the domestic computing power industry.

## Conclusions and Outlook

1. **Computing power becomes the core element of national strategic competitiveness**, major countries around the world are increasing investment in computing power infrastructure, seizing the strategic high ground in the AI era.

2. **The domestic AI chip industry follows a distinctive development path**, forming competitive advantages in large-scale computing power deployment scenarios through cluster breakthroughs, software-hardware integration, and cost-performance advantages.

3. **Computing technology architecture continues to evolve**, with heterogeneous computing, super-node servers, and long-context processing technology becoming important development directions.

4. **Computing power application scenarios continue to expand**, from scientific research paradigm transformation to synthetic biology and embodied intelligence, AI computing power deeply empowers frontier fields.

5. **Computing power infrastructure evolves toward computing-network integration**, future computing power will become universal, on-demand public infrastructure like water and electricity.
