# Computex 2026 AI Chip Highlights

**June 1-5, 2026, Taipei, China** — Computex 2026 (Taipei International Computer Show) concluded this week. Under the theme "AI Together," giants including NVIDIA, Intel, AMD, and Qualcomm unveiled multiple AI compute products. Here is MirrorFrog's roundup of **the most noteworthy AI chip developments this week**.

## ① NVIDIA DGX Station for Windows: Desktop AI Supercomputer

NVIDIA officially unveiled the **DGX Station for Windows** in its Computex 2026 keynote, billed as "the world's most powerful desktop AI supercomputer."

### Core Specs

| Item | Spec |
|------|------|
| **Chip** | GB300 Grace Blackwell Ultra Desktop Superchip |
| **GPU memory** | 252 GB HBM3e (7.1 TB/s) |
| **CPU memory** | 496 GB LPDDR5X (396 GB/s) |
| **Unified memory** | **748 GB** (NVLink-C2C interconnect) |
| **FP4 compute** | **20 PFLOPS** (sparse) |
| **FP8 compute** | 10 PFLOPS (sparse) |
| **Network** | ConnectX-8 SuperNIC, up to **800 Gb/s** |
| **Model capacity** | Can run **1-trillion-parameter** models |
| **System power** | 1,600 W |
| **OS** | Microsoft Windows |
| **Availability** | **Q4 2026** |

**Significance**: DGX Station compresses AI compute (20 PFLOPS FP4) that previously required data-center-class clusters into a single desktop workstation. The 748GB unified memory means developers can run hundred-billion or even trillion-parameter models locally, without relying on the cloud.

---

## ② Intel Crescent Island: Inference-Focused AI GPU

At Computex, Intel detailed its new data-center AI inference GPU **Crescent Island**.

| Item | Spec |
|------|------|
| **Memory** | Up to **480 GB LPDDR5x** |
| **Power** | **350 W** (PCIe form factor) |
| **Precision** | FP4/MXFP4 → FP64 (full precision coverage) |
| **Target** | AI inference workloads (Agentic Inference) |
| **Positioning** | Better price/performance than HBM solutions |
| **Availability** | **H2 2026** |

**Significance**: Crescent Island is Intel's important move in the AI inference market. The 480GB oversized LPDDR5x memory (not HBM) means **cost far below** peer products like NVIDIA H200/B200, targeting enterprise inference deployment scenarios.

---

## ③ Intel Xeon 6+ (Clearwater Forest): Intel's First 18A Data-Center CPU

Intel also launched the new **Xeon 6+ processor** codenamed **Clearwater Forest** — Intel's first data-center CPU built on the **18A process**:

- **288 Darkmont-architecture cores**
- 288MB L2 + 576MB L3 cache
- 12-channel DDR5-8000 memory
- Foveros Direct 3D advanced packaging
- **In the Agent AI era**, the CPU returns to the center of infrastructure

---

## ④ NVIDIA RTX Spark Ecosystem Takes Shape

This week, the **RTX Spark** superchip — NVIDIA's collaboration with **MediaTek** — continued to gain momentum. Multiple OEMs showcased laptop and compact desktop prototypes based on RTX Spark:

- **ASUS**, **Dell**, **HP**, **Lenovo**, **Microsoft Surface**, **MSI** all confirmed launch
- Equipped with **20-core Grace CPU + Blackwell GPU** (6,144 CUDA cores)
- AI compute **1 PFLOPS**
- **Retail availability Fall 2026**

---

## ⑤ Intel × Foxconn AI Infrastructure Partnership

Intel and Foxconn announced a joint AI infrastructure push covering the complete chain from **chip → server → rack-scale systems**, targeting the data-center market opportunity from surging AI inference demand.

---

## ⑥ Domestic AI Chip Developments

Per IDC's 2025 annual report, China's AI accelerator card market shipped approximately **4 million units** in total, with local vendors shipping about **1.65 million units** combined — a market share surpassing **41%**. Huawei's Ascend 950 series has entered mass-production delivery, and Cambricon's MLU690 has begun shipping to internet customers.
