# Computex 2026 Aftermath: The AI PC Chip War Fully Begins

**June 6, 2026** — COMPUTEX 2026 closed yesterday in Taipei, setting a record with **1,500+ exhibitors and 6,000 booths**. The head-to-head clash of the three giants NVIDIA, Intel, and AMD in the AI PC domain became the show's biggest highlight.

## 1. NVIDIA RTX Spark: Officially Launching in June, from $1,399

Less than a week after its COMPUTEX unveiling, the **RTX Spark superchip** — NVIDIA's collaboration with MediaTek — confirmed its launch timeline:

| Key info | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Launch OEMs** | ASUS, Dell, HP, Lenovo, Microsoft Surface, MSI |
| **Availability** | **Fall 2026** |
| **Starting price** | **Not yet announced** (externally estimated $3,000-4,000) |
| **Core config** | Arm CPU (up to 20 cores) + Blackwell GPU (6,144 CUDA) |
| **Unified memory** | **128GB LPDDR5X** (300 GB/s) |
| **Model capacity** | Can run **120-billion-parameter** models, up to 1-million-token context |

**Market reaction**: AMD/Intel/Qualcomm shares came under pressure. Analysts believe RTX Spark will reshape the market from three directions — Windows AI PCs, creator workstations, and edge inference nodes.

---

## 2. Intel 18A Fully in Production: Clearwater Forest + Crescent Island

Intel CEO **Lip-Bu Tan** delivered his first COMPUTEX keynote, bringing two key updates:

### Clearwater Forest (Xeon 6+)
- **288-core** Darkmont architecture
- First **Intel 18A** data-center CPU
- Foveros Direct 3D packaging
- Already in full production

### Crescent Island AI GPU
- **480GB LPDDR5x** memory
- **350W** air-cooled PCIe form factor
- Native FP4 support, targeting agentic inference
- Shipping H2 2026

> Tan said: "As AI moves into the agent era, the CPU is returning to the center of modern AI infrastructure."

## 3. AMD Ryzen AI 400 Series Shipping

AMD showcased the **Ryzen AI 400 series** (Zen 5 + Zen 5C hybrid architecture + XDNA2 NPU) at COMPUTEX:

- NPU compute **60 TOPS**, highest in x86
- 7 consumer models + commercial PRO series
- Multiple OEMs already shipping or about to launch
- **Advancing AI 2026 Summit** (San Francisco) in July
