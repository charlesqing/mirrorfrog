**Huawei Ascend 920 (昇腾 920)** entered **mass production in H2 2025**, marking a **major breakthrough** for China's domestic AI chips. This article analyzes its specs, comparison with NVIDIA H20, the CloudMatrix 384 Ultra system, and its significance for China's AI industry.

{/* truncate */}

## Core Specifications

| Item | Ascend 910C | **Ascend 920** | Improvement |
|------|-------------|----------------|------|
| Architecture | Da Vinci v3 | **Da Vinci v4** | New gen |
| Process | 7nm | **6nm (SMIC domestic)** | More advanced |
| Dies | 2× (dual die) | 2× | Same |
| HBM capacity | ~128 GB | **~96 GB** | Slight down |
| HBM bandwidth | 3.2 Tbps | **4 Tbps** | **1.25×** |
| BF16 compute | 780 TFLOPS | **900+ TFLOPS** | **1.15×** |
| FP16 compute | 1,560 TFLOPS | 1,800 TFLOPS | 1.15× |
| INT8 compute | 3,120 TOPS | 3,600 TOPS | 1.15× |
| TDP | ~310 W | ~400 W | +29% |
| Release | 2025-04 | **2025 H2** | — |

> **4 Tbps bandwidth = the highest domestic HBM bandwidth**, a 25% improvement over Ascend 910C. The 900+ BF16 TFLOPS also exceeds 910C.

## Ascend 920 vs NVIDIA H20 (Head-to-Head)

NVIDIA H20 is a "compliant" AI chip **specially designed for the Chinese market** under US export controls:

| Metric | Ascend 920 | NVIDIA H20 |
|------|------------|------------|
| Positioning | Domestic substitute | China-compliant AI chip |
| Process | 6nm (SMIC) | TSMC 4N (partially domestic after restrictions) |
| Memory | ~96 GB | 96 GB HBM3 |
| Memory bandwidth | **4 Tbps** | 4.0 Tbps |
| BF16 compute | **900 TFLOPS** | 296 TFLOPS |
| **BF16 compute ratio** | **3×** | 1× (baseline) |
| Interconnect | HCCS 1.2 Tbps | NVLink 900 GB/s |
| Software | CANN + MindSpore | CUDA (restricted) |
| Import compliance | ✅ Domestic | ⚠️ US export controls |

> 💡 **Ascend 920 leads H20 significantly in BF16 compute (3×)**, and its 4 Tbps bandwidth matches H20. This is a **key victory for domestic substitution**.

## CloudMatrix 384 Ultra System

Ascend 920 will be used in the **CloudMatrix 384 Ultra** super-node system:

| Item | Configuration |
|------|------|
| **Chip count** | 384 Ascend 920 |
| **Rack count** | 16 (12 compute + 4 network) |
| **Total HBM** | ~36 TB (96GB × 384) |
| **Interconnect** | All-optical mesh, **8,000+ LPO optical modules** |
| **BF16 compute (system)** | **~345 PFLOPS** (est. 900 × 384) |
| **TDP (system)** | ~150 kW |

> **CloudMatrix 384 Ultra system-level BF16 compute ~345 PFLOPS** ≈ **2.4×** NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 cluster (~144 PF FP8 dense).

## Why 920 Is a Key Victory for Domestic Substitution?

### 1. Compute Surpasses H20 for the First Time

| Period | Domestic | NVIDIA China edition | Ratio |
|------|------|---------------|------|
| 2023 | 910B = 320 TFLOPS | H20 = 296 TFLOPS | 1.08× |
| 2024 | 910B = 320 TFLOPS | H20 = 296 TFLOPS | 1.08× |
| 2025 H1 | 910C = 780 TFLOPS | H20 = 296 TFLOPS | 2.6× |
| **2025 H2** | **920 = 900 TFLOPS** | H20 = 296 TFLOPS | **3.0×** |

> **From H2 2025, domestic AI chip compute stably surpasses H20 by 3× for the first time.**

### 2. 6nm SMIC Domestic Process

Ascend 920 uses **SMIC N+1 / N+2 6nm process**:

- ✅ Fully autonomous and controllable
- ✅ Not subject to US export controls
- ⚠️ Yield and cost still behind TSMC 4N

### 3. 4 Tbps Domestic-Highest HBM

Ascend 920's 4 Tbps HBM bandwidth:
- First time domestically reaching the **4 Tbps class** (previous max was 3.2 Tbps)
- Matches H20
- Speculated to use **CXMT (ChangXin) HBM3** or self-developed HBM

### 4. CANN + MindSpore Software Stack

- **CANN 8.x** (Compute Architecture for Neural Networks): the CUDA analog
- **MindSpore 2.4+**: Huawei's self-developed AI framework
- **PyTorch 2.3+ MindSpore backend**: PyTorch compatible
- **vLLM 0.7+ Ascend backend**: low-latency inference
- **ONNX-Runtime Ascend backend**: cross-framework inference
- **Atlas 900/950 series servers**: OEM systems

## Limitations and Challenges

| Limitation | Impact |
|------|------|
| **FP8/FP4 support** | Ascend 920 still mainly BF16/FP16; FP8 optimizing |
| **HBM capacity** | 96 GB below NVIDIA Rubin R200 288 GB / AMD MI400 432 GB |
| **CUDA compatibility** | CANN 8 still needs migration; direct CUDA execution limited |
| **SMIC 6nm yield** | 10-20% lower than TSMC 4N |
| **HBM source** | CXMT HBM capacity limited |
| **Interconnect bandwidth** | HCCS 1.2 Tbps far below NVLink 6 (3.5 TB/s) |
