# NVIDIA Launches RTX Spark: AI Compute Enters the Personal Computer Era

**June 1, 2026, Taipei, China** — In the opening keynote of Computex 2026, NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang officially unveiled the **RTX Spark** superchip, marking NVIDIA's formal entry into the PC processor market long dominated by Intel, AMD, Qualcomm, and Apple.

## RTX Spark: The "Heart" of the Personal AI Computer

RTX Spark was co-developed by NVIDIA and **MediaTek**, using a heterogeneous package of **20-core Grace CPU** + **Blackwell RTX GPU** with **6,144 CUDA cores**. Its AI compute reaches **1 PFLOPS** (one quadrillion floating-point operations per second), meaning for the first time a personal computer has compute comparable to a data-center-class H100 GPU.

| Spec | RTX Spark |
|------|-----------|
| **CPU** | 20-core Grace (MediaTek co-design, Arm architecture) |
| **GPU** | Blackwell RTX (6,144 CUDA cores) |
| **AI compute** | **1 PFLOPS** |
| **Target** | Personal AI agents, local LLM inference |
| **Launch OEMs** | ASUS, Dell, HP, Lenovo, Microsoft Surface, MSI |
| **Availability** | **Fall 2026** |
| **Form factor** | Laptop SoC + compact desktop |

## Jensen Huang's "Full-Stack AI" Strategy

The RTX Spark launch is a key step in NVIDIA's "full-stack AI" strategy. Huang said in his keynote: "AI shouldn't only run in the cloud. Everyone's computer should have the ability to run AI agents."

RTX Spark transforms NVIDIA from a data-center GPU monopolist into a **full-fledged competitor in the personal computing market**. Following the announcement, AMD, Intel, and Qualcomm shares fell.

### Impact on the Market

- **Intel**: its PC AI processor business faces a direct threat
- **AMD**: the Ryzen AI lineup must meet peer-level competition
- **Qualcomm**: the Snapdragon X Elite Copilot+ PC positioning is challenged
- **Apple**: the M-series is no longer the only high-performance AI PC option

## Vera Rubin Platform Enters Mass Production

In the same keynote, Huang also announced that the **NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform has entered full mass production**. Rubin R200 uses a **6-chip CoWoS-L package** (1× Vera CPU + 2× Rubin GPU die + I/O/HBM die), with **288GB HBM4**, **22 TB/s bandwidth**, and **50 PFLOPS FP4** compute (sparse).

The Rubin NVL72 rack (72 Rubin GPUs + 36 Vera CPUs) will **begin shipping in H2 2026**.

## Other Computex 2026 Highlights

- **AMD**: showcased the MI350 series (192GB HBM3e, 5 PFLOPS FP8 dense), officially launching in June
- **Intel**: first public disclosure of Jaguar Shores
- **Qualcomm**: updated AI 200 / 300 series inference card roadmap
- **Domestic AI chip pavilion**: Huawei, Cambricon, Moore Threads and others showcased their latest products

## Industry Significance

The launch of RTX Spark means AI compute is no longer confined to data centers. Individual developers, designers, and researchers will be able to run locally the large-model tasks that previously required cloud GPUs — potentially redefining the market landscape for personal AI computing.

The mass production of Vera Rubin further cements NVIDIA's absolute lead in the data-center AI training domain. Together, the two product lines form NVIDIA's full-stack AI computing map of "cloud training + personal inference."
