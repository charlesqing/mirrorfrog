# Huawei Ascend 950 Mass Production and the Full Picture of China's AI Chip Ecosystem

**June 2026** — The Huawei Ascend 950 series (950PR / 950DT) has entered formal mass-production and delivery — a landmark event for China's AI chip industry in 2026. At the same time, Cambricon's MLU690 has begun shipping and Moore Threads' MTT S5000 specs have been published, formally establishing China's three-polar AI chip landscape.

## Ascend 950 Series: A Historic Breakthrough in Self-Developed HBM

Huawei HiSilicon's **Ascend 950 series** is the fourth-generation Ascend AI chip, first disclosed at Huawei Connect in September 2025 and **formally mass-produced in Q1 2026**.

### 950PR (Prefill inference-only)

| Item | Spec |
|------|------|
| **Architecture** | Da Vinci v5 (SIMD + SIMT dual model) |
| **Process** | N+2 (SMIC domestic) |
| **HBM** | **HiBL 1.0 (Huawei self-developed)**, 128 GB |
| **FP8 compute** | **1 PFLOPS** (HiF8 format) |
| **TDP** | ~400 W |
| **Target** | Inference Prefill (video recommendation, real-time interaction) |

### 950DT (Decode + training)

| Item | Spec |
|------|------|
| **Architecture** | Da Vinci v5 (SIMD + SIMT dual model) |
| **Process** | N+2 (SMIC domestic) |
| **HBM** | **HiZQ 2.0 (Huawei self-developed)**, 144 GB, 4 TB/s |
| **FP8 compute** | **1 PFLOPS** (HiF8 format) |
| **TDP** | ~500 W |
| **Target** | Inference Decode + model training |

### Historical Significance

**Self-developed HBM** (HiBL 1.0 / HiZQ 2.0) is the most important technical breakthrough of Huawei Ascend 950 — the first time a Chinese company has achieved self-developed mass production of HBM memory, completely freeing itself from dependence on SK Hynix / Samsung HBM supply. Combined with the N+2 domestic process, Ascend 950 achieves full-chain domestic production from **HBM → compute die → packaging → system**.

## Cambricon MLU690: The Only Domestic FP8 Support

Cambricon's seventh-generation AI chip **MLU 690 (Siyuan 690)** began mass production and shipment in H1 2026. It is the **first domestically produced AI chip with native FP8 precision support**.

| Item | MLU 690 |
|------|---------|
| **Process** | 5nm (TSMC / SMIC) |
| **FP8 dense** | **2 PFLOPS** |
| **HBM** | **192GB HBM3E, 5 TB/s** |
| **TDP** | ~500 W |
| **Unit price (OAM)** | ~$8,000-12,000 |

MLU 690's FP8 compute (2 PFLOPS dense) is on paper already comparable to NVIDIA Blackwell (B200 FP8 4.5 PFLOPS sparse). Leveraging the financing advantage of its **STAR Market listing**, Cambricon targets 2026 revenue of ¥15-20B (¥7.2B in 2025).

## Moore Threads MTT S5000: From Graphics to Training-Inference Convergence

Moore Threads published detailed **MTT S5000** specs in February 2026, using the 4th-generation MUSA "Pinghu" architecture, with single-card AI compute of **1,000 TFLOPS**, 80GB GDDR6X memory, and 1.6 TB/s bandwidth.

Moore Threads follows a **full-feature GPU** path (graphics rendering + AI compute + general compute), closest to NVIDIA's strategy. The founding team comes from former NVIDIA China, and the **MUSIFY** toolchain helps automatically migrate CUDA code to the MUSA platform, lowering ecosystem migration cost.
