# Huawei Ascend 710

## Product Overview

**Huawei Ascend 710** is an AI chip in Huawei's Ascend series for **lightweight edge and on-device inference**, using the **Da Vinci Lite architecture** with **~35 TOPS INT8** and integrated video codec hardware acceleration. Positioned lighter than the 310P/510, it fits low-power devices such as smart cameras and industrial gateways.

**Strategic position**: As the lightweight member of the Ascend edge lineup, the 710 forms a 35→140→256 TOPS compute ladder with the 310P and 510, covering everything from on-device to edge boxes.
