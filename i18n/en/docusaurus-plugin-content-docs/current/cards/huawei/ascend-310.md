# Huawei Ascend 310

## Product Overview

**Huawei Ascend 310** is the **first AI chip** of Huawei's Ascend series, announced in **October 2018** at the 5th World Internet Conference; together with the Ascend 910 it launched Huawei's "Da Vinci architecture" full-scenario compute system.

The Ascend 310 targets **edge computing and on-device inference**; its standout feature is **8W extreme low power**, pioneering Huawei's unified approach of "one architecture spanning milliwatt to hundreds of watts". It is the core compute source for smart cameras, edge servers, and on-device AI inference, and the technical starting point of Huawei's smart driving chips (Ascend 610 series).

## Key Features

- **8W ultra-low power**: energy-efficiency benchmark of the Da Vinci architecture, fits battery-powered and fanless devices
- **Origin of the full-scenario architecture**: the same Da Vinci architecture flexibly scaled from device to cloud
- **Video codec**: integrated 16-channel full HD hardware decoding
- **Large-scale deployment**: powers dozens of edge hardware products such as Atlas 200 / 500 / 300
