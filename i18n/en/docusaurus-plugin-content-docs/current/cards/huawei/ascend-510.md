# Huawei Ascend 510

## Product Overview

**Huawei Ascend 510** is an AI chip in Huawei's Ascend series for **edge computing and on-device inference**, positioned as the edge-inference workhorse above the Ascend 310P. According to public selection data, **INT8 compute is ~256 TOPS**, with FP16/INT8 mixed-precision support and optimizations for LLM and Transformer inference; it is one of the mainstay models of domestic edge AI boxes (30TOPS+ models).

**Strategic position**: In the edge computing box market contested by Rockchip, Ascend, and AMD, the Ascend 510 covers high-density edge deployments with 256 TOPS INT8, and with the CANN + MindSpore ecosystem it is the first choice for self-reliant edge inference.
