# MetaX XiYun C588

> MetaX Integrated Circuit's flagship iterative model based on XiYun C500/C550, released in September 2024, using self-developed XCORE 1.5 architecture, 12nm domestic process (SMIC), performance targeting international mainstream AI training cards.

## Technical Highlights

- **XCORE 1.5 architecture**: Iterative upgrade of XiHe architecture, optimized for tensor operations
- **12nm domestic process**: SMIC foundry, promoting supply chain domesticization
- **Flagship performance**: As the flagship iteration of C500 series, compute fully exceeds C550
- **Full-process domestic production**: From architecture design to manufacturing and packaging, gradually achieving full domestic production
- **MetaXLink interconnect**: Supports multi-card expansion, building large-scale training clusters

---

## Product Positioning

XiYun C588 is the flagship iterative model of MetaX's C500 series, using XCORE 1.5 architecture and 12nm domestic process, performance targeting NVIDIA A100 80GB. As the transitional flagship between C500 and C600, C588 has significant improvements in compute and memory bandwidth.
