# Enflame L600

> Enflame's fourth-generation training+inference integrated AI chip, based on self-developed Suisi 400 chip, released on July 27, 2025 at World Artificial Intelligence Conference (WAIC), 144GB memory capacity, 3.6TB/s memory bandwidth, 800GB/s interconnect bandwidth, compute performance targeting NVIDIA H20.

## Technical Highlights

- **Fourth-generation training+inference integrated**: Suisi 400 chip, simultaneously supporting AI training and inference, positioned as ultra-high-performance AI computing
- **Ultra-large memory capacity**: 144GB HBM3, supporting 100-billion-parameter large model training and inference
- **Ultra-high memory bandwidth**: 3.6TB/s memory bandwidth, surpassing H20 (4.8TB/s HBM3e actual is 4.8TB/s, L600 is 3.6TB/s)
- **High-speed interconnect**: 800GB/s interconnect bandwidth, supporting large-scale cluster expansion
- **Native FP8 support**: Adapting to new-generation AI model low-precision inference trend
- **Performance surpassing H20**: According to Enflame co-founder and COO Zhang Yalin, L600 compute performance targets and surpasses NVIDIA H20

---

## Product Positioning

Enflame L600 is Enflame's fourth-generation AI chip, positioned as "ultra-high-performance AI computing", mainly used for training clusters and high-performance inference. As a training+inference integrated chip, L600 simultaneously covers training and inference scenarios, targeting NVIDIA H20.

---

## Application Scenarios

- 100-billion-parameter large model training
- High-performance AI inference (LLaMA, GPT, DeepSeek, etc.)
- Training+inference integrated AI computing center
- Domestic H20 replacement scenarios
- Scientific computing and AI for Science
