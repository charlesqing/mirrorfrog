## Technical Highlights

- **Streamlined version of Chunxiao chip**: Reduced core count and memory compared to S80, maintaining PCIe 5.0 support
- **Cost-performance positioning**: Core count reduced by 13%, price estimated to decrease by 33%, targeting mainstream gaming market
- **Full-function GPU**: Also supports graphics rendering, AI computing, and video encoding/decoding
- **MUSA ecosystem**: Supports Moore Threads' complete software stack, including AI frameworks and graphics APIs

---

## Product Positioning

MTT S70 is Moore Threads' graphics card targeting the mainstream gaming market, positioned as NVIDIA RTX 4050 level product. As the "younger brother" of S80, S70 provides a more affordable price while maintaining domestic production and AI capabilities.
