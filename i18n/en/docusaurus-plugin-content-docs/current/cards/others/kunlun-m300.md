# Kunlun M300 (2027)#

## Product Overview#

**Kunlun M300** is a **next-generation AI training/inference chip** released by Kunlunxin Technology on **November 13, 2025** at the Baidu World Conference, with a focus on optimizing **ultra-large-scale, multi-modal large model training and inference**. It is **stronger than M100**, and is planned for **early 2027 market launch**.

M300 is the core product of Baidu Kunlunxin's five-year roadmap (2025-2029), forming a **inference + training** dual-chip layout with M100 (launching early 2026, inference-dedicated).
