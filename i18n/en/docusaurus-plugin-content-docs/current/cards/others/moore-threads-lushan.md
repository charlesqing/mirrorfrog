# Moore Threads Lushan (Fifth-Generation "Huagang" Architecture · Graphics Rendering + AI)

## Product Overview

**Lushan** is Moore Threads' high-performance **graphics rendering chip** built on the **fifth-generation "Huagang" architecture**, with launch planned **within 2026**.

Lushan's key breakthrough is **the first hardware-level ray tracing acceleration engine on a domestic GPU** — previously, Moore Threads' ray tracing capability relied mainly on software paths and general-purpose compute unit emulation. Its sister product "[Huashan](/docs/cards/others/moore-threads-huashan)", also on the Huagang architecture and focused on unified AI training and inference, will also reach mass production within the year; together they form the fifth-generation architecture's "graphics + compute" two-track layout.

At its 2026 interim results briefing, Moore Threads stated that "Lushan"-related GPU products are expected to launch within the year, subject to the company's official product release information. The company's H1 R&D spend was **RMB 769 million (+38.16% YoY)**, with the R&D focus precisely on the Huashan and Lushan chips under the new-generation "Huagang" architecture and related applications.
