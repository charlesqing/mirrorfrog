# Biren BR20X (Next-Generation Flagship Data Center GPU)

## Product Overview

**BR20X** is Biren Technology's **next-generation flagship data center chip**, with **tape-out planned for Q3 2026** and **official commercial launch in the second half of the year**.

Compared with the current BR100 / BR104 (Bili series), the BR20X's upgrade focus is not single-point compute but the **breadth of native support for low-precision data formats** — with an emphasis on enhanced native support for **FP8, FP4**, and other wider data formats, to match the demand of large model inference for low-precision compute.

Biren is one of the last members of the Chinese GPU "**Four Little Dragons**" (Enflame, Moore Threads, MetaX, Biren) to complete capitalization. All four are now listed, and the BR20X is Biren's first flagship product launched after its capital arrival; its tape-out and commercialization cadence is a key window into the iteration capability of high-end Chinese GPUs.

## Next-Generation Chinese GPU Product Timeline

From the second half of 2026 through 2027 is the key window in which next-generation Chinese GPU products land in quick succession and move from tape-out validation to scaled commercial deployment:

| Vendor | Next-Generation Product | Key Milestones | Data Formats |
|------|-----------|----------|----------|
| **Biren** | **BR20X** | **2026-Q3 tape-out, second-half commercialization** | **Native FP8 / FP4** |
| MetaX | Xiyun C700 | 2026 second-half tape-out, 2027 second-half mass production | Adds FP4 |
| Moore Threads | Huashan (AI) / Lushan (graphics) | Mass production / launch within 2026 | 5th-gen Huagang architecture |
| Enflame | 5th-generation cloud AI chip | Announced in 2027 | FP4 support, cross-modal generation |

> **Key insight**: The four vendors' timelines are highly convergent — **tape-out in the second half of 2026, mass production in 2027** is the common rhythm. This shows that the competitive focus of Chinese GPUs has shifted from "who can build it" to "who can tape out on schedule and deliver at scale". For buyers, 2027 is the year high-end Chinese GPUs become truly evaluable, while 2026 remains a transition period dominated by current mass-produced models (Ascend 910C, MLU690, MTT S5000, Xiyun C600).
