# Iluvatar CoreX Tongyong TY1000 (Edge Compute Series)

## Product Overview

The **Tongyong series** is Iluvatar CoreX's product line for **edge compute scenarios**, released in **January 2026**, covering models **TY1000 to TY1200** with **measured dense compute ranging from 100 to 300 TOPS**. Officially, its **performance is claimed to surpass the NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin**.

The design goal of the Tongyong series is to enable models to migrate **from cloud to edge to device without additional operator adaptation** — a key part of Iluvatar CoreX's "unified cloud-edge-device architecture" strategy. In real-world deployments, the Tongyong series can form a **seamless pipeline** with Iluvatar CoreX's **Zhikai series** (cloud inference) and **Tiangai series** (cloud training), supporting an end-to-end compute business chain for industry customers from training to edge deployment.

**Typical scenarios**: real-time control and monitoring analytics in industrial-grade scenarios, as well as local offline inference, industrial quality inspection, private small models, and other edge workloads.
