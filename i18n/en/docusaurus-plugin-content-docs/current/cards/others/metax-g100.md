# MetaX XiCai G100

> MetaX Integrated Circuit's first GPU chip for graphics rendering scenarios, based on self-developed XCORE 2.0 architecture, 14nm process, targeting cloud gaming, cloud desktop and other scenarios, currently in R&D stage.

## Technical Highlights

- **First graphics rendering GPU**: The final product of MetaX's three major product lines (XiYun C series, XiSi N series, XiCai G series)
- **XCORE 2.0 architecture**: Self-developed graphics processing architecture, supporting high-performance graphics rendering
- **Cloud gaming/cloud desktop**: Targeting cloud gaming, cloud desktop, digital twin and other scenarios
- **Fully domestic supply chain**: From architecture design to manufacturing and packaging, gradually achieving full domestic production
- **Compatible with mainstream graphics APIs**: Supports DirectX, Vulkan, OpenGL and other graphics APIs

---

## Product Positioning

XiCai G100 is MetaX's first GPU product targeting the graphics rendering market, positioned for consumer-grade graphics rendering, targeting NVIDIA T1000/T2000. As the final product of MetaX's three major product lines, G100 marks MetaX's completion of "training-inference integration + AI inference + graphics rendering" full-scenario coverage.
