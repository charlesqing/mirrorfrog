# Cambricon MLU690 (Chinese AI Training/Inference Chip)

## Product Overview

**Cambricon MLU690** is Cambricon's flagship chip for the data center AI training and inference market, **in mass production in early 2026**. Using a dual-die chiplet package and the SMIC 5nm-class (N+2) process, its **700+ TFLOPS FP16 compute represents the highest paper specification among Chinese AI chips**; single-card compute is roughly 70% of the NVIDIA H100, reaching 80-90% of the H100 in pure inference scenarios.

**Strategic positioning**: ByteDance is the primary customer (over 500,000 units of the MLU580/590/690 combined purchased), and orders have been secured from leading internet companies such as Alibaba and Baidu. Against the backdrop of US chip export controls on China, the MLU690 is a key domestic alternative in China's large-model training/inference market.

> 📌 **Supernode deployment (WAIC 2026)**: the MLU690 is in mass production, accompanied by the rollout of a **256-card scale supernode cluster solution** already deployed in multiple intelligent computing centers — marking the move of Chinese chips from "single-card compute" toward "system-level compute".

## MLU690 vs MLU590 vs H100

| Metric | MLU690 | MLU590 | H100 SXM | Gap (vs H100) |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------------|
| Process | TSMC 4nm (dual die) | 7nm chiplet | TSMC 4nm | On par |
| Memory | 196GB HBM3 | 96GB HBM2e | 80GB HBM3 | +145% |
| Memory Bandwidth | 3.35 TB/s | ~2 TB/s | 3.35 TB/s | On par |
| FP16 | 700+ TFLOPS | 345 TFLOPS | 989 TFLOPS | ~71% |
| INT8 | 2,800+ TOPS | 1,380 TOPS | 1,979 TOPS | +41% |
| TDP | ~500W | ~350W | 700W | -29% |
| Interconnect | MLU-Link 890Gbps | MLU-Link | NVLink 4 900GB/s | Nearly on par |
| Software ecosystem | NeuWare | NeuWare | CUDA | Gap remains |

> **Key insight**: the MLU690's memory capacity is 2.45x that of the H100, and its INT8 inference compute surpasses the H100 by 41%. Its shortcomings mainly come from FP16 training compute and interconnect efficiency in distributed training beyond a thousand cards.

## Process and Supply Chain

- **Foundry**: after TSMC halted supply due to the Entity List, production shifted entirely to SMIC N+2 (5nm-class). The MLU690 is one of the largest customers for SMIC N+2
- **Yield**: Sigmaintell estimates SMIC's 5nm yield at about 34% in 2025Q1, with 40%+ possible by Q4
- **Capacity booking**: N+2 capacity is locked in through 2027
- **Next generation**: tentatively the MLU790 / MLU690 successor, expected to ship in 2026Q4 and ramp in 2027

## Smart Driving: MLU610

Cambricon has also launched the MLU610 chip for smart driving scenarios, which has won orders from BYD, Li Auto, and others, expanding into the edge/automotive AI market.

## September 2026 Update: Pricing and Market Dynamics

Reuters reported on September 10, 2026 that Cambricon repriced its next-generation processor, provisionally MLU690, 20-30% above indicative levels from two months earlier, driven by rising HBM costs, with an official guide price around 190,000 yuan in September 2026. Peer vendors including MetaX and Iluvatar CoreX were reported to have raised prices over the same period. These are reported channel and guidance figures rather than disclosed transaction prices, and they are not entered into the specification table. The move matters for procurement because it narrows the historical discount of domestic accelerators against export-compliant NVIDIA parts; compare on total cost with the [TCO calculator](/docs/tco-calculator) rather than sticker price alone.

## November 8, 2026 Update: Inventory and Cash Flow Behind the Q3 Numbers

Financial-press analyses of the Cambricon and Hygon Q3 filings in early November fill in the balance-sheet side that the income statement omits. Cambricon: first-three-quarter revenue 9.526 billion yuan, net profit 3.715 billion yuan, operating cash flow +228 million yuan (cumulative -2.712 billion yuan over 2023-2025), inventory 11.665 billion yuan, prepayments 3.044 billion yuan, notes payable 4.966 billion yuan, receivables 938 million yuan (9.8% of revenue), 502 million yuan of inventory write-downs, roughly 771 days of inventory turnover and inventory at 52.2% of total assets. Hygon: revenue 14.955 billion yuan, net profit 2.847 billion yuan, operating cash flow -2.443 billion yuan (versus +2.255 billion a year earlier), inventory 8.921 billion yuan, prepayments 5.885 billion yuan, receivables 6.026 billion yuan (40.3% of revenue), and contract liabilities falling from 2.019 billion yuan at the start of the year to 11.89 million yuan.

> Sourcing note: these are press compilations of quarterly financial filings, not vendor-published product specifications or shipment volumes, and the specification table is unchanged. The two models differ: Cambricon collects quickly downstream and uses supplier credit upstream while parking cash in inventory; Hygon carries cash on both receivables and prepayments as customer deposits contract. Inventory could be material locked in for the 690 ramp and 2027 orders, or a demand-forecast miss — Q4 to Q1 2027 deliveries decide which. For buyers, a supplier's balance-sheet health is a better signal of long-term supply stability than its quoted price.
