# Intel Arc Pro B50

## Product Overview

**Intel Arc Pro B50** is an entry-level professional workstation GPU based on the **Xe2 (Battlemage) architecture**, released by Intel in **May 2025** at COMPUTEX Taipei. It features **16GB GDDR6 memory**, **70W TDP**, **no power connector** (draws power directly from PCIe slot), and **half-height, half-length** design. It is an ideal choice for **compact workstations, edge AI, and multi-display** applications.

**Strategic Position**: In Intel's professional GPU product line, the Arc Pro B50 is the **entry-level professional card for 2025-2026**, positioned below the Arc Pro B60. Compared to the Arc Pro B60, it has **lower TDP** (70W vs 200W) and **smaller size** (half-height half-length vs dual-slot full-height), making it the preferred choice for **compact workstations and edge AI**.

## Key Features

- **70W Low TDP**: No additional power connector required, draws power directly from PCIe slot
- **Half-height, half-length design**: Suitable for compact workstations and edge devices
- **16GB GDDR6 memory**: Capacity meets most AI inference and professional graphics needs
- **Professional drivers**: Optimized for AutoCAD, Solidworks, Maya, Photoshop, and other software
- **Multi-display support**: Supports multiple monitor outputs
