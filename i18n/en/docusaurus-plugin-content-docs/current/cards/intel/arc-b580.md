# Intel Arc B580

## Product Overview

**Intel Arc B580** is a mid-range desktop graphics card from Intel based on the **second-generation Xe2 (Battlemage) architecture**, released in Q4 2024 and primarily targeting the 1440p gaming market. It features **12GB GDDR6** memory, **160 XMX AI engines**, **233 TOPS INT8** peak compute, and a **190W TBP**, with an MSRP of $249. Thanks to its large 12GB memory and strong value, it has become a budget entry card for AI and content creation.

**Strategic position**: the B580 is one of Intel's most competitive products in the discrete GPU market; its 12GB of memory exceeds the same-priced RTX 4060 (8GB), and combined with the OpenVINO / IPEX ecosystem it offers a price-performance advantage in local AI inference and creation scenarios.

## Key Features

- **12GB GDDR6 large memory**: exceeds 8GB competitors at the same price
- **160 XMX AI engines**: 233 TOPS INT8, supports local AI inference
- **XeSS 2**: AI upscaling, enhanced gaming performance
- **OpenVINO / IPEX support**: Intel ecosystem AI acceleration
- **AV1 encode/decode**: dual media engines
