# Intel Arc Pro B60

## Product Overview

**Intel Arc Pro B60** is a professional workstation GPU based on the **Xe2 (Battlemage) architecture**, released by Intel in **May 2025** at COMPUTEX Taipei. It features **24GB GDDR6 memory**, **12.28 TFLOPS FP32** compute, **160 XMX AI engines**, and **197 TOPS INT8** compute. It supports **multi-GPU expansion** (up to 8 cards), providing a combined **192GB of memory**, capable of running AI models up to **150 billion parameters**. It is a **cost-effective professional card** for **AI development, deep learning, and 3D rendering**.

**Strategic Position**: In Intel's professional GPU product line, the Arc Pro B60 is the **mid-range professional card for 2025-2026**, positioned between the consumer-grade Arc B580 and high-end data center GPUs. Compared to the NVIDIA RTX 6000 Ada, it offers **larger memory capacity** (24GB vs 48GB, but at a lower price), making it the preferred choice for **budget-sensitive AI developers**.

- **24GB GDDR6 memory**: Capacity is 2× that of consumer-grade Arc B580 (12GB)
- **160 XMX AI engines**: Optimized for AI compute
- **Multi-GPU expansion**: Up to 8 cards, 192GB total memory
