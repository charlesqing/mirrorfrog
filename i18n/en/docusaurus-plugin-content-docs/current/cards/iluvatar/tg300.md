# Iluvatar CoreX TianGai 300 (BI-V300)

## Product Overview

**TianGai 300** is the **first product built on Iluvatar CoreX's newly developed in-house architecture**, and the newest flagship of the company's TianGai general-purpose GPU training line. **Iluvatar CoreX (9903.HK) officially unveiled it on July 19, 2026, during the 2026 World Artificial Intelligence Conference (WAIC 2026)** — the company's first flagship iteration in the five years since TianGai 100 launched in 2021.

The chip is built on Iluvatar CoreX's **in-house SIMT general-purpose compute architecture**, integrating **more than 100 billion transistors** and **more than 200 AI compute cores**, each with its own high-speed cache and data path. Single-chip **FP16 compute exceeds 1,000 TFLOPS and INT8 compute reaches over 2,000 TOPS**, built on a **7nm process** with typical power kept **under 600W**. It supports **FP4, FP8** and other precisions along with the MMA, DPX, and FMA instruction sets, and ships in **both PCIe card and OAM module** form factors.

Where earlier parts simply stacked up compute, TianGai 300 differentiates on **how efficiently raw compute converts into tokens actually produced**. Officially disclosed measured results include: **Attention efficiency above 90%** across precisions; **10% higher Attention throughput than the Hopper architecture reference at 64k context**; **over 70% compute efficiency in DeepSeek V4 MoE scenarios** and 10% higher average inference efficiency than Hopper; **time-to-first-token roughly 20% lower than the Hopper reference** across Qwen, GLM, Kimi, and DeepSeek; and **average communication latency 13% lower** during Decode. The company's overall positioning is to **match and exceed Hopper**-class international solutions.

Worth noting: Morgan Stanley's supply-chain research at WAIC recorded that TianGai 300's **Prefill and Decode performance exceeds the NVIDIA H100** (company's own claim), while **management simultaneously acknowledged the part still trails Blackwell by a generation**. Iluvatar CoreX has already scheduled the **next-generation TianGai 400 to tape out in 2027**, explicitly targeting Blackwell.

> ⚠️ **HBM generation discrepancy**: two accounts of TianGai 300's memory technology exist. The **Baidu Baike entry records HBM3**, while **Morgan Stanley's supply-chain research records HBM3E**. Neither Iluvatar CoreX's official news release nor mainstream outlets (The Paper, Southern+, China Securities Journal) **specify the HBM generation**, publishing only 144 GB of capacity and roughly 4 TB/s of bandwidth. This card follows the **HBM3E** figure from Morgan Stanley's independent supply-chain research — on the grounds that a 144 GB / ~4 TB/s combination sits closer to HBM3E's production maturity — while flagging the disagreement. Treat the vendor's final data sheet as authoritative.

## Key Features

### 1. A SIMT General-Purpose Architecture: Back to the Essence of Computing

- TianGai 300 is the **first product on Iluvatar CoreX's new in-house architecture**, whose SIMT design **efficiently supports scalar, vector, and tensor compute types**
- The company stresses that it is **"not confined to the Transformer architecture"** — beyond Attention, FFN, and MoE, it also targets **matrix solving, dynamic programming, sparse computation, and digital twins**
- Three dedicated instruction extensions:
  - **ixSMEX** — raises data reuse to cut redundant memory traffic
  - **ixDPX** — compresses computation that previously took six instructions in dynamic programming **into a single instruction**
  - **ixTrans** — lossless matrix transposition that lowers storage conflicts and memory overhead

### 3. Three Intelligences as the Design Thread

Iluvatar CoreX's head of AI and accelerated computing, Shan Tianyi (单天逸), frames AI's evolution in three stages, and these form the product's design rationale:

- **Information intelligence** (understanding and generating information): heavy optimization of **Attention and MoE**, raising the parallelism between matrix and vector compute to cut data-read waits and redundant computation; algorithm and hardware co-optimization targets the expert-scheduling and data-routing losses in MoE
- **Action intelligence** (planning and execution): strengthened **PD disaggregation**, with differentiated optimization of the Prefill and Decode stages; for Decode's high-frequency, multi-card, small-message communication pattern, the design cuts protocol overhead and optimizes link paths and data movement
- **Exploratory intelligence** (modeling, prediction, discovery): a return to the essence of computing, with scalar, vector, and tensor support across FP4, FP8 and other precisions plus MMA, DPX, and FMA instructions — serving materials discovery, weather prediction, digital twins, and world models

### 4. Ecosystem and Deployment

- TianGai 300 **is ready for scaled deployment**, with completed deep adaptation alongside domestic cloud providers, server OEMs, interconnect partners, and supernode systems — supporting anything from a single machine to large clusters
- Iluvatar CoreX has been building out its software stack since 2018 and has now developed **close to a hundred acceleration libraries**, plus a toolchain spanning **communication, compilation, drivers, quantization, and performance analysis**
- Support for mainstream global model frameworks, models, and key acceleration components reduces customers' repeated work on migration, adaptation, and performance tuning
- Built on the **DeepSpark open-source community**, co-building applications and evaluation platforms with partners
- The **next-generation TianGai 400 is planned to tape out in 2027**, targeting NVIDIA Blackwell
