# NVIDIA N1X (PC Processor)

## Overview

**NVIDIA N1X** is NVIDIA's **first own-brand processor for the PC market**, marking its most direct challenge yet to the x86 PC processor space long split between Intel and AMD. The chip was **co-developed with Microsoft**, derived from the **RTX Spark platform**, and targets consumer and OEM Windows PCs with a planned **Fall 2026 launch**.

The October 4, 2026 announcement moved markets immediately: **Intel and AMD shares each fell about 4.2%** that day — investors view NVIDIA's entry, backed by its manufacturing relationships, chip design expertise, and the brand authority built through AI and gaming hardware, as a structural threat rather than a fringe experiment.

N1X inherits its foundations from RTX Spark: an **Arm CPU + Blackwell GPU unified memory architecture** positioned as an "Agentic AI" PC platform — running large models and AI agents natively on local Windows devices. This puts it in direct competition with Qualcomm's Snapdragon X Elite route into Arm-based Windows PCs, but NVIDIA differentiates through the completeness of its GPU ecosystem (CUDA, DLSS, the RTX gaming and creator stack).

## Core Specifications

| Item | Spec |
|------|------|
| **Architecture** | Arm CPU + Blackwell GPU (RTX Spark derived) |
| **Partners** | Microsoft (platform & Windows integration), MediaTek (CPU architecture, industry-speculated) |
| **Market Positioning** | Consumer / OEM Windows PCs (laptops + desktops) |
| **Unified Memory** | Same architecture family as RTX Spark (unified LPDDR5X) |
| **TDP** | Not disclosed |
| **Availability** | **Fall 2026** |
| **Announced** | 2026-10-04 (official reveal) |

> ⚠️ **Spec note**: NVIDIA has not yet disclosed N1X core counts, compute, memory capacity, or pricing. The table above lists confirmed information only; further details are expected as the Fall 2026 launch approaches.

## Market Impact

| Dimension | Impact |
|------|------|
| **For Intel / AMD** | A third major player enters PC processors for the first time; both stocks fell ~4.2% on announcement day |
| **For Qualcomm** | Arm Windows PC market shifts from "Qualcomm-only" to multi-player competition |
| **For Apple** | Apple Silicon's "unified memory + custom architecture" route gains its strongest Windows-side counterpart |
| **For OEMs** | Dell, HP, Lenovo, Asus, MSI, and Microsoft Surface gain a new silicon supply option |

## What to Watch

1. **AI PC competition shifts up a level**: N1X's pitch is not raw performance but "running large models / agents locally," continuing RTX Spark's platform narrative of 120B-parameter models with 1M-token context
2. **x86 moat under test**: Windows application compatibility, OEM adoption willingness, and real-world value for money will decide whether N1X converts market share
3. **Key signals ahead**: independent benchmark results and flagship OEM design wins are the most direct evidence of N1X's real competitiveness
