# NVIDIA H200 (Hopper Refresh)

## Product Overview

The NVIDIA H200, released in 2024, is a memory-upgraded version of the H100. The key change is the upgrade from 80GB HBM3 to **141GB HBM3e**, with bandwidth increasing from 3.35 TB/s to **4.8 TB/s** (+43%). This delivers significant performance gains for 70B+ parameter model inference, enabling single-GPU loading of longer contexts.

## November 2026 Update: Import Approvals and Secondary-Market Pricing

Reuters reported on November 5 that roughly 600,000 H200 accelerators are sitting in warehouses in Hong Kong, China, because mainland authorities have not approved large-scale imports. U.S. rules permit exports to approved Chinese customers, but only narrow licences have been issued — Alibaba, ByteDance and Tencent each received about 10,000 units for training use only. The same reporting puts eight-GPU H200 servers above 6 million yuan on the secondary market, roughly three times the price a year earlier. Follow-up reporting on November 7 says about 24 Chinese companies have been cleared to buy or handle H200 chips, with some registered offices apparently vacant and ownership links to restricted entities, raising questions about licence due diligence.

All of the above is press reporting, not a first-party regulatory or NVIDIA document; the stockpile size, prices and company list are unconfirmed and are not entered into the specification table.
