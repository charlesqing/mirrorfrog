# NVIDIA RTX Pro 4000 Blackwell

## Product Overview

The **NVIDIA RTX Pro 4000 Blackwell** is a professional workstation GPU launched by NVIDIA in 2025 based on the **Blackwell architecture**, positioned as the entry model of the RTX Pro series. With **24GB GDDR7 ECC memory** and a **145W TDP** (plus an SFF single-slot 70W version), it retains 5th-generation Tensor Cores and FP4 inference capability, targeting lightweight AI development and professional graphics.

## Key Features

- **24GB GDDR7 ECC memory**: local inference of 14B-30B models
- **5th Gen Tensor Cores**: FP4 inference support
- **Professional drivers + ECC**: the choice for workstation stability
- **145W low power**: SFF version at 70W fits small chassis
