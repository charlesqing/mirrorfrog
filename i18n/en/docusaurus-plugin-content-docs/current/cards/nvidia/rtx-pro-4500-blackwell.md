# NVIDIA RTX Pro 4500 Blackwell

## Product Overview

The **NVIDIA RTX Pro 4500 Blackwell** is a professional workstation GPU launched by NVIDIA in 2025 based on the **Blackwell architecture**, positioned as the mid-range model of the RTX Pro series. With **32GB GDDR7 ECC memory** and a **200W TDP**, it retains 5th-generation Tensor Cores and FP4 inference capability, targeting mainstream AI development and professional graphics workloads.
