# NVIDIA H20 (2024)

## Product Overview

**NVIDIA H20** is an **export-compliant AI accelerator** launched by NVIDIA for the **China market** in early 2024, based on the **Hopper architecture** (same as H100/H200).

Due to U.S. export controls (October 2023 updates), NVIDIA deliberately **reduced H20's computing power** to comply with regulations:
- **FP16**: 148 TFLOPS (~15% of H100)
- **FP8**: 296 TFLOPS (~15% of H100)
- **Memory**: 96GB HBM3 (bandwidth 4.0 TB/s)

H20's positioning: **Preserve NVIDIA's ecosystem advantages while complying with export rules**, competing with domestic Chinese AI chips (Hygon DCU, Kunlun, MetaX, etc.).

## Export Control Background

### U.S. Export Control Timeline

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2022-10 | U.S. BIS issued export controls, restricting A100/H100 exports to China |
| 2023-10 | New rules further tightened, added "performance density" limits |
| **2023-11** | **NVIDIA launched H20 / L20 / L2** (compliant versions) |
| 2024-02 | H20 started shipping to Chinese customers |
| **2025-07** | **NVIDIA notified distributors H20 will be discontinued** (export controls tightened again) |

### Compliance Limit Parameters

| Parameter | Limit Threshold | H20 Actual Value |
|-----------|-----------------|------------------|
| **FP16 Compute** | < 300 TFLOPS | 148 TFLOPS |
| **Performance Density** | < certain threshold | Compliant |
| **Memory Bandwidth** | No explicit limit | 4.0 TB/s (not reduced) |

> **Strategy**: NVIDIA chose to **preserve memory bandwidth** (important for LLM inference), while **severely reducing compute power**, to maintain competitiveness within compliance constraints.

## Comparison with Domestic Chinese Chips

### H20 vs Hygon DCU K100

| Metric | NVIDIA H20 | Hygon DCU K100 | Difference |
|--------|------------|----------------|-----------|
| **FP16** | 148 TFLOPS | **192 TFLOPS** | K100 +30% |
| **Memory** | 96GB HBM3 | 64GB HBM3 | H20 +50% |
| **Bandwidth** | 4.0 TB/s | 3.2 TB/s | H20 +25% |
| **Ecosystem** | CUDA (full) | ROCm (compatible) | H20 advantage |
| **Supply** | Discontinued July 2025 | Stable supply | K100 advantage |
| **Price** | ~$25k (est.) | ~$15k (est.) | K100 cheaper |

### H20 vs Kunlun P800

| Metric | NVIDIA H20 | Kunlun P800 | Difference |
|--------|------------|--------------|-----------|
| **FP16** | 148 TFLOPS | **345 TFLOPS** | P800 2.3× |
| **Memory** | 96GB HBM3 | 32GB HBM3 | H20 +200% |
| **Bandwidth** | 4.0 TB/s | Not public | H20 advantage |
| **Ecosystem** | CUDA | Baidu Paddle | Each has advantages |

> **Conclusion**: H20 still has advantages in **memory capacity and bandwidth**, but **compute power has been surpassed by domestic chips**. With H20's discontinuation in 2025, domestic substitution is accelerating.

## Use Cases

- ✅ **NVIDIA Ecosystem Migration Transition** (CUDA code requires no modification)
- ✅ **Large Model Inference** (96GB memory + 4.0 TB/s bandwidth advantage)
- ✅ **Short-term Projects** (can still purchase before July 2025)
- ❌ **Long-term Training Clusters** (discontinued in 2025, supply chain risk)
- ❌ **Cost-performance Priority** (domestic chips have higher compute, lower price)
- ❌ **Autonomous Control Requirements** (U.S. government can tighten export controls at any time)

## Discontinuation Impact (July 2025)

In July 2025, NVIDIA notified distributors that **H20 will be discontinued**, reasons:
1. U.S. export controls may tighten further
2. H20 profit margins compressed (deliberately reduced compute)
3. NVIDIA prioritizing capacity for H200/B200

**Impact on China market**:
- Existing H20 customers: Short-term no impact, long-term maintenance/expansion difficult
- Prospective customers: Accelerating shift to domestic chips (Hygon, Kunlun, MetaX, etc.)
- Domestic substitution process: **Accelerated by 1-2 years**
