# NVIDIA H800 (2023)

## Product Overview

**NVIDIA H800** was NVIDIA's **first export-compliant AI accelerator** for the **China market**, based on the **Hopper architecture**, launched in **early 2023**.

Due to U.S. export controls (October 2022 version), NVIDIA deliberately **reduced H800's NVLink interconnect bandwidth** to comply with regulations:
- **FP16**: 1,979 TFLOPS (sparse, **same as H100**)
- **FP8**: 3,958 TFLOPS (sparse, **same as H100**)
- **Memory**: 80GB HBM3 (bandwidth 3.35 TB/s, **same as H100**)
- **NVLink**: **Reduced** (H100 900 GB/s → H800 ~400-600 GB/s)

H800's positioning: **Preserve compute power and memory, reduce interconnect bandwidth**, to comply with initial export rules.

## Export Control Background

### U.S. Export Control Timeline (H800 Related)

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2022-10 | U.S. BIS issued initial export controls, restricting A100/H100 exports to China |
| **2023-03** | **NVIDIA launched H800** (reduced NVLink bandwidth to comply) |
| 2023-10 | U.S. issued **stricter export control rules** |
| **2023-10** | **H800 also banned**, stopped shipping to China |

### H800 vs H100 Compliance Reduction Comparison

| Parameter | H100 | H800 | Reduction |
|-----------|------|-------|------------|
| **FP16 Compute** | 1,979 TFLOPS | 1,979 TFLOPS | **No reduction** |
| **FP8 Compute** | 3,958 TFLOPS | 3,958 TFLOPS | **No reduction** |
| **Memory** | 80GB HBM3 | 80GB HBM3 | **No reduction** |
| **Memory Bandwidth** | 3.35 TB/s | 3.35 TB/s | **No reduction** |
| **NVLink Bandwidth** | 900 GB/s | ~400-600 GB/s | **Reduced ~33-55%** |
| **TDP** | 700W | 350W | **Reduced 50%** |

> **Strategy**: NVIDIA chose to **preserve compute and memory** (important for single-card inference), while **reducing interconnect bandwidth and power** (impacts large-scale training clusters).

## H800 vs H20 Comparison

| Metric | H800 | H20 | Difference |
|--------|------|-----|-----------|
| **FP16** | 1,979 TFLOPS | 148 TFLOPS | H800 13.4× |
| **FP8** | 3,958 TFLOPS | 296 TFLOPS | H800 13.4× |
| **Memory** | 80GB HBM3 | 96GB HBM3 | H20 +20% |
| **Bandwidth** | 3.35 TB/s | 4.0 TB/s | H20 +19% |
| **NVLink** | ~400-600 GB/s (reduced) | 900 GB/s (reduced) | Similar |
| **TDP** | 350W | 400W | H20 +14% |
| **Lifecycle** | 2023.03 - 2023.10 (only 7 months) | 2024.02 - 2025.07 | H20 longer |

> **Conclusion**: H800 had much higher compute than H20, but extremely short lifecycle (only 7 months). H20 is NVIDIA's **second-generation compliant product** after the October 2023 new rules.

## Use Cases (Historical Review)

- ✅ **Short-term procurement in 2023** (no longer available for purchase)
- ✅ **Single-card inference** (compute not reduced, 80GB large memory)
- ❌ **Large-scale training clusters** (NVLink bandwidth reduced, multi-card interconnect performance degraded)
- ❌ **Procurement after 2024** (discontinued, no supply)

## Discontinuation Impact (October 2023)

In October 2023, the U.S. issued **stricter export control rules**, and H800 was also banned:

**Impact on China AI industry**:
- Existing H800 customers: Short-term no impact, but cannot expand capacity
- Prospective customers: Forced to shift to **H20** (compute drastically reduced) or **domestic chips**
- Domestic substitution process: **Accelerated by 1-2 years**
