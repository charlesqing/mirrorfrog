# AMD Instinct MI500 Series (CDNA 6)

## Product Overview

The **AMD Instinct MI500 series** is the **next-generation flagship AI accelerator** after the MI400 series (CDNA 5). It was formally previewed by AMD CEO Lisa Su at **Advancing AI 2026 (2026-07-23, San Francisco)**, with an **official launch expected in 2027**.

The defining trait of the MI500 is that it is **jointly defined and developed by AMD and OpenAI**: based on the real compute demands of its frontier large models, OpenAI feeds AMD operational data such as workload characteristics, memory capacity, interconnect bandwidth, and power efficiency, which in turn guide the chip architecture, memory specifications, and software stack adaptation. According to Lisa Su, from the 2023 MI300X to the 2027 MI500, AMD aims to achieve a **thousandfold leap in AI performance within four years**.

> 🔮 **Status: forward-looking / in development**. The specifications below are based on the architecture generation and year disclosed at Advancing AI 2026; **concrete compute, memory capacity, TDP, etc. have not yet been announced** — final figures are subject to AMD's official announcements.

## The OpenAI Joint Development Model

The MI500 is AMD's first chip product **jointly defined with a leading AI lab (OpenAI)**:

- Since October 2025, OpenAI and AMD have had a multi-year, multi-generation strategic agreement that locks in a combined **12GW+** GPU purchase commitment
- OpenAI will deploy a total of **6GW** of AMD Instinct GPUs, with the first 1GW based on the **MI450 series** (online in 2026 H2)
- AMD has issued OpenAI warrants for up to **160 million** shares of common stock (strike price $0.01/share); if fully exercised, OpenAI would hold about **10%** of AMD
- Based on real large-model workloads, OpenAI feeds back requirements on **memory capacity, interconnect bandwidth, and power efficiency** to guide MI500 architecture design
