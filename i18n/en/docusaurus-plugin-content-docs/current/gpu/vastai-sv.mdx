# Vastai Semiconductor SV Series

**Vendor**: Vastai Semiconductor

**Category**: GPU Graphics Processor

**Architecture**: Vastai

## Introduction

Vastai Semiconductor high-end GPU chips with two generations of proprietary GPU cores. SV series covers three product lines: graphics rendering GPU, data center AI GPU, and edge AI GPU. One of the few domestic vendors with commercial deployment in both general AI and rendering. SV chip deep learning inference throughput reaches 2× that of general-purpose GPUs.

## Specifications

| Model | Compute | Memory | Interface | TDP | Process |
|------|------|-----------|------|-----|------|
| VA12 | 160 TFLOPS (FP16) / 512 (INT8) | 32GB HBM2e | PCIe 4.0 | 250W | 7nm |
| VA1L | 72 TFLOPS (FP16) / 200 (INT8) | 16GB GDDR6 | PCIe 4.0 | 150W | 7nm |
