# Enflame Blaze T20/T21

**Vendor**: Enflame Technology

**Category**: ASIC Dedicated Accelerator

**Architecture**: Enflame DTU

## Introduction

Enflame Technology focuses on cloud AI chips with four generations of proprietary architectures and five chips. Blaze T20/T21 are training and inference products covering chips, accelerator cards, intelligent computing clusters, and a complete software platform. In January 2026, the company received STAR Market IPO acceptance.

## Specifications

| Model | Compute | Memory | Interface | TDP | Process |
|------|------|-----------|------|-----|------|
| T21 | 128 TFLOPS (FP16) / 256 (INT8) | 32GB HBM2E (1.6 TB/s) | OAM | 300W | 7nm |
| T20 | 134.4 TFLOPS (FP16) / 268.8 (INT8) | 32GB HBM2E (1.6 TB/s) | PCIe 4.0 | 300W | 7nm |
